# Business Questions

## Primary question

How can an online learning platform identify engagement patterns, academic risk, and factors associated with student success?

## Question families

### Engagement
- How active are students across courses and time?
- Which activity patterns are associated with stronger or weaker outcomes?
- How does engagement change before assessments?

### Academic performance
- What assessment patterns are observed across courses?
- How are completion, scores and submission timing distributed?
- Which learner or course characteristics are associated with performance differences?

### Retention
- What patterns are visible among withdrawn students?
- How does inactivity relate to withdrawal?
- Are there identifiable temporal patterns around registration and withdrawal?

### Risk
- Can combinations of low engagement, late assessment activity and low performance identify groups requiring attention?
- Which indicators are descriptive versus predictive?

## Analytical discipline

The project must distinguish:
- observation;
- association;
- prediction;
- causation.

We do not call an association a cause without appropriate evidence.
