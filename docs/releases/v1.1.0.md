# OULAD — EdTech Learning Analytics v1.1.0

**Release status:** Prepared release notes — not yet published  
**Release scope:** Completed investigative work through AN6  
**Proposed release commit:** The merge commit of PR #17, after review and approval

## Overview

Version 1.1.0 records the completion and integration of the OULAD project's investigative analytics work through AN6. It consolidates the documented outcomes of engagement-sensitivity analysis and recorded VLE activity-pattern analysis on top of the existing PostgreSQL data-engineering foundation.

This is an evidence and repository milestone. It is not a production analytics release and does not claim that the full analytical roadmap is complete.

## Included work

### AN5 — Engagement Sensitivity

AN5 evaluated how alternative treatments of repeated `student_vle` source records affect recorded-activity measures. Five read-only SQL investigations were executed and reviewed, producing eight retained result sets (623 rows) and eight visual summaries.

The investigation compared three metric-only scenarios:

- **R0:** retain every source row.
- **R1:** collapse exact duplicates across all six source columns.
- **R2:** retain the maximum `sum_click` within each five-column candidate-key group.

The returned scenario click totals were 39,605,099 (R0), 38,343,063 (R1), and 34,402,738 (R2). Overall any-window activity coverage was 89.68% in each scenario. Median within-presentation rank correlations remained high; the minimum top-decile Jaccard overlap was 0.8286.

AN5 established that activity-presence measures were invariant across R0/R1/R2 in the tested comparisons, while click-volume measures remained sensitive to repeated-row treatment. R0 was retained as the source-row baseline for downstream presence/day/resource measures. R1 and R2 remain sensitivity alternatives, not source-cleaning rules.

### AN6 — Recorded VLE Activity Patterns

AN6 defined and measured recorded activity within the course-relative presentation window, using one registered student-presentation as its analytical grain:

`(code_module, code_presentation, id_student)`

Four read-only investigations returned six complete, untruncated result sets.

Key reconciled findings:

- 32,593 registered student-presentations across 22 module-presentations.
- 28,500 registrations had in-course recorded activity: **87.44%** pooled in-course coverage.
- 728 registrations had recorded activity only outside the in-course window.
- 3,365 registrations had no recorded activity in any observed window.
- The 10,655,280 source activity rows reconciled into 9,966,292 in-course rows (93.53%) and 688,988 pre-course rows (6.47%); no post-course bucket was returned.
- All 10,655,280 activity rows matched resource metadata. No unmatched resource rows, missing `activity_type` rows, or unmatched-registration activity rows were returned.

The 87.44% in-course coverage is distinct from AN5's 89.68% any-window coverage. AN6 retained zero-activity registrations in its distribution denominators and distinguished outside-window-only activity from no recorded activity.

## Data and engineering foundation

The project retains seven RAW and seven typed STAGING tables. The documented row counts reconcile between the two layers. Source data remains preserved: no source-row deduplication or deletion was applied, and the non-unique `student_vle` candidate key remains an explicit modelling limitation.

The historical quality registry records 29 tests: 25 PASS, 4 REVIEW, and 0 FAIL. The four REVIEW findings remain documented; they have not been silently reclassified or treated as resolved by the analytical investigations.

## Interpretation boundaries and known limitations

- Recorded VLE rows describe platform records. They do not directly measure learning quality, attention, effort, comprehension, achievement, or causal effects.
- A missing activity match means that no matching record was found under the tested source, key, and time window. It does not establish that no learning occurred.
- Repeated `student_vle` rows have unresolved source semantics. Click totals and row-volume comparisons remain sensitive to their treatment.
- AN5's R0/R1/R2 scenarios are sensitivity analyses, not approved deduplication or source-repair rules.
- AN6's seven-day bins are relative reporting periods, not academic weeks. VLE `week_from` and `week_to` metadata remain incomplete.
- Activity-type row volume is not equivalent to unique-student reach; reach across activity types is non-additive.
- No production KPI, composite engagement score, causal claim, intervention threshold, or predictive feature has been approved.
